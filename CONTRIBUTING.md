# Contributing to WorldSign

1. Identify the sign language and regional variety precisely.
2. Provide a gloss, meaning, usage context and source.
3. Obtain informed consent from the signer for the intended publication and reuse.
4. Record the exact media licence or permission terms.
5. Submit entries as `needs-review`; never self-approve.
6. Community reviewers should verify linguistic form, meaning, regional variation and ethical handling before publication.

Do not scrape third-party sign dictionaries or bulk-copy their images/videos. A public webpage is not automatically a redistribution licence.
